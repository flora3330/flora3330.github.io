Global trends and gaps in human–wildlife conflict research

Conducted a global analysis of human–wildlife conflict literature to evaluate research priorities, geographic patterns, and alignment with conservation needs. Used R for data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualization to surface taxonomic biases, geographic gaps, and disparities between where research attention goes and where species conservation needs are greatest.

HIV drug-resistance mutation explorer

Built an interactive visualization for researchers and clinicians to explore how HIV reverse transcriptase (RT) mutations drive resistance to commonly used RT inhibitor drugs. Combines mutation prevalence data with resistance profiles into one explorable interface, making it easier to reason through complex mutation patterns when weighing treatment and research decisions.
